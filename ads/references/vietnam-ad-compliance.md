# Vietnam advertising compliance reference

Not legal advice. Everything here is **verify against primary source** (the
text on thuvienphapluat.vn, vbpl.vn or the Official Gazette) before it goes into
an ad, a plan or a client message. Retrieved 2026-10-02 from the secondary
sources cited per item; none of these was checked word for word against the
signed text.

Shared legal text: the hub keeps the blog-side Vietnamese compliance notes in
`claude-blog/skills/blog/references/vi-compliance.md` (sibling checkout in the
hub; read it when present). It is the shared source for the sponsored and
affiliate disclosure sentences and the functional-food disclaimer. This file
does not repeat that text; it adds what paid ads need. When the two disagree,
stop and tell the operator which one needs the primary-source check.

## 1. Advertising Law amendment, effective 2026-01-01

- Law No. 75/2025/QH15 amends the Law on Advertising; effective 2026-01-01.
  Source: Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism article
  https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/luat-quang-cao-nam-2025-va-tac-dong-toi-doanh-nghiep-quang-cao-so-20260131233648735.htm
  and https://diendandoanhnghiep.vn/luat-quang-cao-2025-siet-trach-nhiem-nguoi-noi-tieng-trong-quang-cao-10168362.html.
- Reported content: celebrities, KOLs, KOCs and influencers who present an ad
  carry legal responsibility and are expected to check the advertiser and the
  product documents; false advertising can mean penalties, correction and
  compensation. Tighter rules for online and cross-border advertising.
- Not confirmed from the sources read: the required disclosure wording, which
  article sets it, and the fine amounts. Do not quote an article number.
  Practical rule used in this repo: mark paid influencer and affiliate content
  visibly as advertising ("Quảng cáo", "Hợp tác cùng [thương hiệu]") and
  keep the brand's product documents on file.

## 2. Decree 342/2025/ND-CP (implements the Law)

- Issued 2025-12-26. Effective date: one source says 2026-02-15 (luatvietnam),
  another article text reads 2025-02-15 (probably a typo). Verify.
  Sources: https://luatvietnam.vn/linh-vuc-khac/diem-moi-cua-nghi-dinh-342-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-quang-cao-883-107004-article.html,
  https://baoquangninh.vn/tu-15-2-quang-cao-my-pham-khong-duoc-gay-hieu-nham-nhu-thuoc-chua-benh-3391338.html.
- Reported points: pop-up style ads need a one-click close (video at most 5
  seconds before closing is allowed); online ad service providers must remove a
  violating ad within 24 hours of an official written request; foreign and
  domestic ad service providers register contact details with the Ministry of
  Culture, Sports and Tourism; 11 groups of special products and services have
  extra content rules.
- Whether Decree 342/2025 replaces Decree 181/2013/ND-CP, and whether the
  pre-confirmation procedures listed below survive unchanged, was **not
  confirmed**. Until verified, treat pre-approval as required wherever it is
  listed in section 3.

## 4. Claims to avoid in copy

Treat as banned unless the operator shows documents (**verify**; the Law on
Advertising lists prohibited content, read the primary text):

- Cure, treat or prevent a disease (cosmetics, functional food, services).
- Guaranteed results or "100 percent" outcomes; before and after photos for
  health and cosmetic results without documents.
- Superlatives and comparisons ("tốt nhất", "số 1", "duy nhất") without a
  verifiable basis; naming or disparaging a competitor.
- Doctor, pharmacist or hospital imagery or endorsement for products in
  section 3.
- Fake urgency, fake reviews, fake sale counters, fabricated celebrity use.

Write real ad copy with full Vietnamese diacritics.

## 5. Platform policy overlap

Meta, TikTok and Google each have their own health, cosmetics, financial and
personal-attributes policies that apply in Vietnam on top of local law, and
they change often. Do not state a platform rule from memory. Load the
platform audit and creative specs (`meta-audit.md`, `tiktok-audit.md`,
`compliance.md`, `compliance-requirements.md`), check the live policy page, and
report which of the two (law or platform) is the stricter for this ad. Each
platform also has a paid-partnership or branded-content label: use it for
influencer ads in addition to the visible disclosure.

## 6. What the agent does

1. Identify the product category from the request before planning copy.
2. If it is in section 3 (including any cosmetics shop), put "needs content
   confirmation before any ad runs" at the top of the plan with the authority
   and the documents to gather, and mark it unverified where the table does.
3. Never write a banned claim, even when the operator asks; offer a compliant
   alternative.
4. Never state a fine, article number or deadline as fact. Quote the source
   and the retrieval date.
5. Ad copy is advertising, not a blog post: the blog gates do not score it.
